# Internal Question References

The 2026, 2025, 2024, and 2023 MAO National Convention Mu Individual sets are withheld while publication permission is pending. Their 118 retained records live in `muNationals.ts`, ordered by those four years, with stable IDs but no bank numbers. Previously omitted questions remain omitted.

Use these records only as internal editorial benchmarks when assigning difficulty, topic, and method labels to newly added questions. Compare the mathematical work required, not merely the contest year or position. Preserve the existing canonical topic/method vocabulary and the 1-5 difficulty scale.

Do not import this directory into application code, catalogs, routes, public bundles, Trainer, multiplayer, or tutor endpoints. Do not copy the withheld questions into another public catalog. Publication requires a new explicit permission decision; never restore them merely to fix a test or fill a numbering gap.

Public Mu questions are numbered consecutively from 1 in the approved catalog. Their IDs and original-source credit numbers are separate and must not change when display numbering changes. Preserve stored progress, bookmarks, and history rather than deleting them.
